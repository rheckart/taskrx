TaskRx is a privacy-first medication reminder app built for people who cannot afford to miss a dose. It stores your medication schedule and dose history locally on your Android device, so reminders fire on time even when you are offline, in Doze mode, or the app is closed.

Add medications by searching the free RxNorm drug database, set daily or interval schedules, and mark doses taken or skipped with a single tap. When your device is back online, TaskRx syncs your data to the cloud so it is available across your devices.

This proof-of-concept release validates the React Native + Expo + Convex + Clerk + Notifee + expo-sqlite stack for reliable, offline-first medication reminders on Android.

Key features in this POC:
- Magic-link sign-in via Clerk
- RxNorm medication search
- Daily and interval schedules
- Local notifications that survive Doze and app closure
- Dose logging with persistent local history
- Background sync to Convex when online
- Cross-device visibility for medications and dose logs

TaskRx does not diagnose conditions or provide medical advice. Always follow the guidance of your healthcare provider.
